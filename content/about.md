If you are reading this, you are probably one of my sons, and many years have passed since I wrote it. I wanted you to know who I was before you were old enough to ask.

## Who I am

Write your name, your age as you write this, the work you do, and what an ordinary day looks like. Small details are the ones a son will treasure most: what you eat for breakfast, the song you hum, the way you take your tea.

## Where I come from

Describe the place you grew up, the house, the street, the people. Who raised you? What did they teach you without saying a word?

## What I believe

Write about the things you hold true: how to treat people, how to work, what to do when things go wrong. You do not need to be wise. Be honest.

## What I hope you remember

::: letter
My dear sons, whatever you become, I hope you will be kind first and clever second. I hope you will tell the truth when it costs you something. And I hope you will come back to this page whenever you miss me.
:::

Everything else I have to say is in the Blog and the Journal. Start wherever you like.
