---
title: An evening walk (sample)
date: 2026-09-27
tags: [walk, daily, sample]
---

Walked for an hour as the light went orange and then grey. Nobody was in a hurry. A man was selling roasted nuts from a cart, and the smell followed me for two streets.

I noticed things I normally miss: a cat asleep on a wall, a window with a lamp already lit, two children arguing seriously about a game only they understood.

I came home tired in the good way, and slept well.
