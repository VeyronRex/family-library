---
title: Rain and tea (sample)
date: 2026-10-19
tags: [weather, daily, sample]
---

It rained all afternoon, the kind of rain that makes everything slow down. I made tea, sat by the door and watched the street turn into a river.

Rain always takes me back to being a boy, running outside to get wet while someone shouted at me to come in. I did not come in for a long time.

I think I will go out in the next rain, just to remember what it feels like.
