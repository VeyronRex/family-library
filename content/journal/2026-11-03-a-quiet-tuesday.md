Woke before the alarm. The house was still, and I stood at the window with my tea for a long time doing nothing at all, which I am trying to do more often.

Work was ordinary. The best part of the day was a short call with an old friend who made me laugh until I had to put the phone down. I should call him more.

In the evening I cooked rice and lentils, and the kitchen smelled the way my mother's used to. Small days like this are the ones I do not want to forget.
