The first week of learning anything is miserable. You are slow, you are clumsy, and everyone around you seems to be better. I used to quit in that week. Now I know it is the price of the ticket.

Here is what has helped me:

1. Decide how small the first step is, then make it smaller.
2. Practise every day, even for ten minutes.
3. Teach what you learned to someone else. It will show you what you did not understand.
4. Be kind to the beginner you are.

> You do not need to be good at it yet. You only need to be there tomorrow.

When you are learning something and want to give up, come back and read this. I wrote it for you.
