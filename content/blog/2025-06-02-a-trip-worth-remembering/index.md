Every trip has one moment you will want to tell your children about. Begin with that moment, and then go back to the start.

![The sun coming up over a calm sea](sunrise.svg "Put the photograph's story here. The picture file sits in the same folder as the post.")

## Where we went

Name the place, the date and who was with you. Say how you got there, and what you expected. Expectations are fun to read afterwards.

## What I noticed

The smell of the market. The sound of the train. A stranger who gave directions and then walked part of the way with us. Write down the small things, because they are the ones that disappear first.

## What it taught me

Travel is the quickest way I know to find out what you are afraid of and what you can do without. I came home with fewer opinions and more questions, which I think is the right way round.

::: memory
At dawn, the sea was so still that the boats looked as if they were resting on glass. Nobody spoke. It was the quietest moment of the whole trip, and the one I remember best.
:::
