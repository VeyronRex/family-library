I am not a wise man, but I have been wrong often enough to notice patterns. These are three that I would like you to keep.

## One: ask the question you are afraid to ask

Almost every time, the person you were afraid to ask was glad you did. Most of what I wasted years on could have been solved in a short conversation.

## Two: money is a tool, not a score

Earn it honestly, spend it on what matters, and save some for the day you will need it. Do not let anyone, including yourself, measure you by it.

## Three: look after your body

You will not feel its value until it is hurting. Walk every day, sleep enough, and see the doctor when something is wrong.

::: lesson
Nobody remembers the meetings you attended. They remember how you made them feel, and whether you were there when it mattered.
:::

That is all for now. There will be more lessons, and I will probably contradict some of these.
