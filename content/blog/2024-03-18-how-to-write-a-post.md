Writing a post is just writing. You do not need to think about design: the library takes care of how everything looks. Here is a quick tour of what you can use.

## Headings

A line that starts with `##` becomes a heading, like the one above. Use `###` for a smaller one.

## Emphasis and lists

Put stars around a word to make it *italic* or **bold**. Start lines with a dash to make a list:

- the first thing
- the second thing
- the third thing

Use numbers when the order matters:

1. Wake up early.
2. Write one true sentence.
3. Go for a walk.

> A quote looks like this. Start the line with a greater-than sign.

## Links and pictures

A [link](https://example.com) is written with square brackets, then round brackets. A picture is the same with an exclamation mark in front. If you add words in quotes after the file name, they become the caption.
