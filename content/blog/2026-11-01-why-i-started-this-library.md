Some things are too important to leave to memory. I started this library because I realised that I could not tell you what my father's voice sounded like, and I did not want that to be true for you.

So here are the stories, the lessons and the ordinary days, written down while I still remember them clearly. Some will be funny. Some will be hard to read. All of them are true.

## What you will find here

The **Blog** holds longer pieces: journeys, lessons, and the things I have learned the hard way. The **Journal** holds my days as they come, written quickly and without polish.

## A promise

I will not make myself look better than I was. If I made mistakes, you will read about them, because a mistake explained is worth more than a success admired.
